Projects

Selected data and policy projects, including research collaborations.

Historical U.S. Union Elections Database

I am helping to build a historical database of 13,673 National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) union elections dating back to 1936. The project uses agentic AI for document matching, text extraction, and archival research to identify election locations, industries, and outcomes from historical NLRB records, successfuly assigning counties to 90% of cases. We are developing a publicly accessible repository with downloadable data, technical documentation, and interactive visualizations, and the resulting dataset aims to support research on the geographic and industrial distribution of union organizing in the United States.

OpenAudit

Together with my collaborators, I am helping to build a national database of Commission on Audit (COA) reports on Philippine local governments, extracting fiscal data locked in scanned audit reports. The project uses agentic AI to extract, validate, and structure data from thousands of unstructured Philippine COA reports. The resulting dataset contains 12,031 local government-year records from 2016-2022 on auditor-issued Notices of Disallowance, a measure of the potential misuse of public funds. The database supports research on local public finance, audit findings, and fiscal accountability.

Satellite-Based Poverty and Income Mapping for the Philippines

I trained a machine learning algorithm using satellite imagery and official government data to estimate the household income and poverty incidence for all 42,000 barangays in the Philippines for 2026, extending municipal-level statistics one administrative level down. It combines nighttime lights, satellite embeddings, building footprints, and land cover features in gradient-boosted models trained on PSA statistics, validated with spatial block cross-validation, and benchmarked to official city and municipal data.
