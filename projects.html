Projects

Selected data and policy projects, including research collaborations.

OpenAudit

An agentic AI pipeline that converts the Philippine Commission on Audit’s archive of annual audit reports, more than 30,000 published since 1998, into a structured and analyzable dataset. Coordinated LLM agents extract text from PDF, DOCX, XLSX, and scanned documents requiring OCR, classify auditor findings against a 14-rule schema, and adversarially validate each extraction against the source document. As a proof of concept, the pipeline produces 12,031 local-government-year records of unsettled Notices of Disallowance for 2016 to 2022, verified at 97.9% accuracy against two independent random samples, adding roughly ₱1.95 billion (US$32 million) in previously unrecorded disallowances to the public record.

Satellite-Based Poverty and Income Mapping for the Philippines

I trained a machine learning algorithm using satellite imagery and official government data to estimate the household income and poverty incidence for all 42,000 barangays in the Philippines for 2026, extending official municipal-level statistics one administrative level down. It combines nighttime lights, satellite embeddings, building footprints, and land-cover features in gradient-boosted models trained on official PSA statistics, validated with spatial-block cross-validation and benchmarked to official city and municipal figures.
