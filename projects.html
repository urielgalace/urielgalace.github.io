Projects

Selected data and policy projects, including research collaborations.

OpenAudit

Collaboration to build a national database of Commission on Audit reports for Philippine local governments, extracting fiscal data locked in PDF audit reports and coordinating with public officials to expand access to government data. The database supports research on local public finance, audit findings, and accountability.

Satellite-Based Poverty and Income Mapping for the Philippines

Small-area estimates of household poverty incidence and average income for all roughly 42,000 barangays in the Philippines, extending official municipal-level statistics one administrative level down. Combines nighttime lights, satellite embeddings, building footprints, and land-cover features in gradient-boosted models trained on official PSA statistics, validated with spatial-block cross-validation and benchmarked to official city and municipal figures.
