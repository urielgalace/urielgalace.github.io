Projects

Selected data and policy projects, including research collaborations.

Barangay Income and Poverty Atlas

I built a website presenting a new dataset of household income and poverty incidence for all 42,010 barangays in the Philippines for 2025, extending municipal-level statistics one administrative level down. To do this, I trained machine learning models using satellite imagery, census data, official government statistics, and door-to-door household surveys. Household income is estimated with a blend of gradient boosting and ridge regression, and poverty incidence with gradient boosting. The models were validated on held-out provinces and survey reports, and the estimates were benchmarked to official regional and provincial figures and the Philippine Statistics Authority’s 2023 municipal small-area estimates.

OpenAudit

Together with my collaborators, I am helping to build a national database of Commission on Audit (COA) reports on Philippine local governments. The project uses agentic AI to extract, validate, and structure data from thousands of these unstructured COA reports, with the resulting dataset containing 12,035 local government-year records from 2016-2022 on auditor-issued Notices of Disallowance, a measure of the potential misuse of public funds. The database aims to support research on local public finance and fiscal accountability.

Historical U.S. Union Elections Database

I am helping to build a historical database of 13,673 National Labor Relations Board (NLRB) union elections dating back to 1936. The project uses agentic AI to match union election records to historical NLRB decisions, extract information on election locations, industries, and outcomes, and conduct archival research to fill in the gaps. It has so far identified counties for 90% of cases. We are developing a publicly accessible repository with downloadable data, technical documentation, descriptive statistics, and interactive visualizations, and the resulting dataset will aim to support research on the geographic and industrial distribution of union organizing in the United States.
